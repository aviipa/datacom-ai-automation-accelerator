Slide 2 — Proposed Agentic AI Architecture

Introducing "OrderBot": Our Autonomous Order Agent

An intelligent digital teammate that perceives, plans, validates, and routes orders 24/7 in under 5 minutes.

2 / 3
1. Trigger (24/7) Email & PDF Detected

OrderBot watches inbox API and parses PDF attachments in seconds.

→
2. Perception Parallel System Checks

Queries Salesforce account status & Google Sheets stock via APIs.

→
3. Planning Smart Decision Engine

Evaluates confidence (≥98%), credit standing, and stock availability.

→
4a. Happy Path Auto-Confirm & Sync

Reserves stock, updates Salesforce, emails customer & warehouse (<5 min).

/
4b. Guardrails Exception Escalation

Instant rep clarification or Ops approval for credit holds / low confidence.

Goal

Process hardware orders from email receipt to confirmation in < 5 minutes at 99.5% target accuracy.

Perception Tools

Email Reader API, PDF Structured Extractor, Salesforce Read API, and Google Sheets Read API.

Action Tools

Email Sender (customer, warehouse, sales rep), Salesforce order update, and atomic stock decrement.

Memory & Learning

Logs every outcome to spot recurring issues (e.g., reps using outdated forms) and improve accuracy over time.
